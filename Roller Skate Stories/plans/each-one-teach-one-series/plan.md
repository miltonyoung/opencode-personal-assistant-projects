# Plan: Each One Teach One Skate Series

## Goal and intention

- **Outcome:** Build a recurring, community-led adult roller skate learning event at Skateland Chandler that converts new and non-skaters into regulars who love skating and feel welcome in the AZ skate community.
- **Why it matters:** Skating has a social barrier — rinks can feel intimidating for beginners. A structured, peer-teaching event lowers that barrier, creates belonging, and grows the local adult skate scene. It also feeds content and audience growth for Roll N Glide and Roller Skate Stories.
- **Who it is for:** New and non-skating adults in the Phoenix area; the broader AZ roller skate community; Roll N Glide + Roller Skate Stories as co-hosts.
- **Domain tag:** skating / business / community
- **Kairos / kronos / both:** Both. The TikTok/Instagram response (254 likes, 56 comments in <24 hours) makes this a kairos moment. If it works, it becomes a kronos monthly rhythm.

## Done criteria

- [ ] Skateland Chandler approves a 1.5-hour teaching slice during an existing adult night, with a group rate of $15/admission+rental and a discounted teacher rate of $5/teacher.
- [ ] Roll N Glide + Roller Skate Stories confirm roles, promotion split, and financial handling.
- [ ] First event scheduled, ticketed at $25/beginner, with 20–40 paid sign-ups.
- [ ] 10–20 seasoned skaters recruited and briefed as volunteer teachers.
- [ ] Event runs successfully: beginners learn basic skills, no major safety incidents, positive feedback collected.
- [ ] Follow-up path in place: next event announced, group chat or mailing list created, recap content published.
- [ ] Decision made on whether to continue monthly and how to scale.

## Risks and unknowns

- **Teacher shortage:** Demand may exceed 20 teachers. Mitigation: cap attendance or switch to station/pod model.
- **Skateland terms:** They may not accept $15 group rate or $5 teacher rate. Mitigation: have fallback numbers ready; emphasize zero-risk upside for the rink.
- **No-shows:** Pre-paid tickets reduce but don’t eliminate this. Mitigation: clear refund/exchange policy.
- **Safety/liability:** Beginners fall. Mitigation: teach safe falling first; confirm waiver coverage; consider requiring helmets for absolute beginners.
- **Weather/holidays:** Mid-December event may conflict with holidays. Mitigation: pick date with Skateland early; have January backup.
- **Financial loss if under-attended:** Mitigation: set minimum signup threshold (e.g., 20) and cancellation/refund policy.
