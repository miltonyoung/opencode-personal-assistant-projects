What Is the Portrait Bar?
Premium, affordable photo activation
Built on the same gear and workflow as GenIdem, a Phoenix-area portrait-booth service. Closer to a mini studio setup than an iPad selfie booth.
  1. Guests step into the portrait area.
  2. They press a handheld trigger to take their own photo.
  3. Professional camera and strobe deliver flattering, high-quality images.
  4. Within 24-72 hours, the host receives a private online gallery link.
  5. Guests scan a QR code to download their photos once the gallery is live.

Day-Of Operations
What happens at the event
  1. RSS operator arrives before the party to set up the Portrait Bar.
  2. Guests use the handheld trigger to take photos during the party.
  3. Operator stays nearby to assist, troubleshoot, and manage the line.
  4. After the party, gear is packed and cleared quickly.
  5. Photos are culled and edited; gallery link is sent to the host within 24-72 hours.
  6. RSS can create short recap clips for social media with permission.
