Roller Skate Stories
Go do something you love.

Like the Boys & Girls Club for the roller skating community — a place where skaters of every age find belonging, family, security, and something to look forward to, just by showing up with their skates on.

Our Mission
Roller Skate Stories is about preserving the heart of the culture: the people, the sessions, the struggles, and the joy — and giving skaters a home where their stories matter.
Why We Exist

Most skaters leave the rink with only a memory. RSS closes that gap — so every skater, from day one to year thirty, has proof they were there and belongs to something bigger than the session.

Who It’s For

Everybody. You don’t need a home rink, influencer status, elite skill, or a social media platform.

If you are taking on the challenge of skating and want community while you do it, this is for you. Once you skate, you are part of it.

What You Get

Belonging. A community that sees you, not your follower count.

Perspective. See yourself and the culture from angles you never asked for.

Proof. Your story becomes part of the living history of skating.

Ways to Get Involved
