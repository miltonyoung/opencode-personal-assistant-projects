# Portrait Bar — Next Steps After Successful Pitch

**Status:** Pitch accepted by Skateland Chandler  
**Date:** 2026-11-06  
**Owners:** Marcus Dover (primary), Milton Young (secondary)

---

## What just happened

The Skateland manager approved the RSS Portrait Bar partnership. They want to move forward.

## Immediate next step

Skateland will identify dates that have several birthday parties booked. RSS will be invited to set up the Portrait Bar as a **test / content shoot**.

The purpose of the test shoot is to:
- Capture real photos and video of the Portrait Bar in action
- Create advertisement content for:
  - Skateland’s website
  - Skateland’s Instagram and social channels
  - RSS channels
- Prove the operational workflow before regular bookings begin
- Produce a gallery and recap that can be shown to future party hosts

## What needs to be ready before the test shoot

1. **Portrait Bar equipment** — camera, lighting, trigger, backdrop, power
2. **Booth check-in system** — so guests can tag themselves to a party before stepping in (see `portrait-bar-checkin-system.md`)
3. **Operator** — the person running the booth that day
4. **Content capture plan** — what photos and video need to be captured for marketing
5. **Photo release / usage rights process** — so Skateland and RSS can use the images
6. **Post-event workflow** — edit, deliver gallery, create recap content

## Open questions to resolve before the test shoot

- What date(s) is Skateland proposing?
- How many parties will be present on the test date?
- What time can RSS arrive to set up?
- Where exactly will the booth be located?
- Does Skateland need a photo release signed by guests or party hosts?
- Who will be the RSS operator and assistant for the test shoot?

## Deliverables after the test shoot

- Edited photo gallery from the event
- Short promotional video or reel
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Any operational lessons learned
- Updated proposal or one-sheet for Skateland’s website
