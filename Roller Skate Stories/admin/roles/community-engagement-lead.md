# Role: Community Engagement Lead

**Scope:** Roller Skate Stories (RSS) and Roll N Glide (RNG)  
**Reports to:** Media Lead and Positivity Lead (dotted line to both)  
**Owner of:** Day-to-day community interaction across Discord, Instagram, and TikTok  
**Time commitment:** 5–8 hours/week at low volume; can scale up if accounts grow

---

## Purpose

The Community Engagement Lead is the friendly, consistent voice that keeps RSS and RNG alive in people’s feeds and chats. They make sure no comment goes unanswered, new media gets posted promptly, and the online community feels as welcoming as the in-person one.

This person is the bridge between the content the Media team produces and the people who consume it.

---

## Core responsibilities

### 1. Instagram engagement and posting

For both **@rollerskatestories** and **@rollnglide** accounts:

- Post **at least one story per day** on each account when active
- Post new media (photos, videos, reels) when provided by the Media team
- Write captions in the brand voice
- Respond to comments and DMs within 24 hours
- Engage with followers’ posts, partner accounts, and local skater accounts
- Flag standout community members or content ideas to the Media Lead

### 2. TikTok engagement and posting

For both RSS and RNG TikTok accounts:

- Post new videos when media becomes available
- Respond to comments within 24 hours
- Engage with skate-related TikTok trends and creators
- Adapt Instagram content for TikTok when appropriate

### 3. Discord community moderation and engagement

For the **Roll N Glide Discord** (and any future RSS Discord):

- Welcome new members
- Keep conversations friendly and on-topic
- Mediate disagreements before they escalate
- Share announcements, events, and new content
- Answer common questions or route them to the right person
- Report any serious issues to the Positivity Lead or owners

### 4. Feedback loop

- Track which posts and stories get the most engagement
- Share weekly insights with the Media Lead
- Suggest content ideas based on what the community responds to
- Surface community questions or concerns to Lane Leads

---

## What this role is not

- This role does **not** create original photo/video content from scratch. They post and distribute media created by the Media team.
- This role does **not** approve partnerships, pricing, or official statements. They escalate those to owners.
- This role does **not** handle serious conflict or bans alone. They work with the Positivity Lead.

## Time estimates (per week)

| Activity | Time per day | Time per week |
|----------|--------------|---------------|
| Daily Instagram stories (2 accounts) | 10–20 min | 1–2.5 hours |
| Comment/DM responses (2 IG + 2 TikTok) | 15–30 min | 2–3.5 hours |
| Discord moderation and engagement | 15–30 min | 2–3.5 hours |
| Posting new media when available | variable | 30 min–1 hour |
| Weekly insights/reporting | — | 15–30 min |
| **Total** | | **5–8 hours/week** |

For a volunteer, this is a meaningful commitment. If the person has limited time, the scope can be reduced — for example, start with one brand or one platform.

---

## Success metrics

- Response time to comments and DMs stays under 24 hours
- Stories are posted daily on active accounts
- Discord feels active and welcoming
- Engagement rate trends upward or stays healthy
- Community feedback reaches Lane Leads regularly

---

## First 30-day onboarding path

### Week 1: Access and brand voice

- Get access to IG, TikTok, and Discord accounts
- Read the RSS and RNG brand guides
- Review the last month of posts and comments to understand tone
- Read the RSS operations manual and mission-to-operations document
- Meet with the Media Lead and Positivity Lead

### Week 2: Shadow and assist

- Post stories under supervision
- Practice responding to comments with suggested replies
- Observe Discord patterns and member personalities
- Draft a weekly posting rhythm

### Week 3: Run independently

- Take over daily posting and engagement
- Handle comments and DMs solo
- Run Discord engagement independently
- Submit first weekly insights report

### Week 4: Optimize

- Review what worked and what did not
- Adjust posting rhythm based on data
- Propose one new engagement idea
- Check in with Media Lead and Positivity Lead

---

## Good first tasks

1. **Audit current social presence** — list all accounts, passwords/access status, last post date, follower counts.
2. **Brand voice cheat sheet** — write 5 example captions for RSS and 5 for RNG in the right tone.
3. **Discord welcome flow** — review how new members are currently greeted and improve it.
4. **One week of stories** — plan and post 7 days of Instagram stories for one account.
