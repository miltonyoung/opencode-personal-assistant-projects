# Coordinator Orientation Call — Agenda

**For:** Marcus Dover and Milton Young when onboarding new coordinators.  
**Duration:** 45–60 minutes per coordinator.  
**Goal:** Make the coordinator confident enough to take their first assignment and know when to escalate.

---

## Before the call

- [ ] Coordinator has been invited and confirmed interest
- [ ] Coordinator has received the onboarding packet:
  - `admin/coordinator-onboarding.md`
  - `admin/operations-manual.md`
  - `admin/rss-mission-to-operations.md`
  - `admin/current-tasks.md`
- [ ] Calendar invite sent with video/call link
- [ ] Marcus and Milton know which coordinator role to offer (A or B)

---

## Call agenda

### 1. Welcome and why them (5 min)

- Thank them for saying yes.
- Explain why you thought of them: organized, reliable, good with people.
- Frame the role as foundational — they are helping build the operating system of RSS.

### 2. What RSS is and why it matters (10 min)

- Read or summarize the mission:
  > Roller Skate Stories is about preserving the heart of the culture: the people, the sessions, the struggles, and the joy — and giving skaters a home where their stories matter.
- Explain the six lanes: Movement, Media, Merch, Music, Events, Positivity.
- Share the recent win: Skateland approved the Portrait Bar partnership.
- Explain the immediate need: RSS has 20+ interested people but no system to organize them yet.

### 3. The coordinator role (10 min)

- A coordinator is the person who keeps the task machine running.
- They do **not** decide vision, spend money, or approve partnerships.
- They **do**:
  - Keep the task board clean and current
  - Make sure every task has an owner and deadline
  - Follow up with contributors when things stall
  - Run the weekly digest and review
  - Escalate blockers to Marcus and Milton

### 4. Boundaries and escalation (10 min)

**Coordinators can:**
- Assign tasks
- Set and change deadlines with contributor input
- Follow up and nudge
- Run meetings
- Update the task board
- Ask Lane Leads for updates

**Coordinators cannot:**
- Spend money
- Approve partnerships or pricing
- Promise anything to outside partners
- Overrule a Lane Lead or owner decision
- Do creative/event work themselves unless explicitly asked

**Escalation path:**
- Day-to-day questions → Operations Coordinator or other coordinator
- Lane-level decisions → Lane Lead
- Money, partnerships, public promises, conflicts → Marcus or Milton

### 5. Tools and workflow (10 min)

- Walk through the task lifecycle:
  ```
  Idea → Triage → Ready → Assigned → In Progress → Review → Done
  ```
- Explain the lanes and how tasks are tagged.
- Show the current task list.
- Explain the weekly rhythm:
  - Monday digest
  - Mid-week check-ins
  - Friday review

### 6. First assignment (5 min)

- Give the coordinator their first task.

**Coordinator A:**
> Research and recommend a task-tracking tool for RSS. Evaluate 2–3 options. Present pros/cons and a clear recommendation. Due [date].

**Coordinator B:**
> Build the first contributor roster from the 20+ “Get Involved” form responses. Tag each person by lane interest and flag the top 6–10 candidates for first outreach. Due [date].

- Confirm they understand the deliverable.
- Confirm they know who to ask if they get stuck.

### 7. Questions and next steps (5 min)

- Ask: *What questions do you have?*
- Ask: *What do you need from us to be successful?*
- Confirm the next check-in time.
- End with encouragement.
