# Onboarding Plan: Community Engagement Lead

**Role:** Community Engagement Lead for Roller Skate Stories and Roll N Glide  
**Goal:** Get this person fully running daily social engagement and Discord moderation within 30 days.

---

## Before day one

- [ ] Marcus or Milton personally invites the person and explains the role
- [ ] Confirm they can commit 5–8 hours/week (with option to scale up later)
- [ ] Send them the onboarding packet:
  - `admin/roles/community-engagement-lead.md`
  - `admin/operations-manual.md`
  - `admin/rss-mission-to-operations.md`
  - `roll-n-glide/brand/rng-brand-identity.md`
  - `roll-n-glide/operations/rng-event-operations-manual.md`
- [ ] Schedule the orientation call

---

## Orientation call agenda (45–60 min)

### 1. Welcome and why them (5 min)

- Thank them for joining.
- Explain why you thought of them: positive energy, good with people, already engaged with comments.
- Frame the role as the online face of RSS and RNG.

### 2. RSS and RNG context (10 min)

- RSS mission: preserve culture, serve people, make stories visible.
- RNG mission: bring people together through partner skating.
- Explain that RSS is managing RNG operations.
- Share recent wins: Skateland Portrait Bar partnership, RNG first event Nov 21.

### 3. The role (15 min)

Walk through `admin/roles/community-engagement-lead.md`:
- Instagram: daily stories, post new media, respond to comments/DMs
- TikTok: post videos, respond to comments
- Discord: welcome, moderate, engage, escalate
- Feedback loop: weekly insights report

### 4. Boundaries (10 min)

**They can:**
- Post media provided by the Media team
- Respond to comments and DMs
- Welcome and engage Discord members
- Suggest content ideas
- Escalate issues

**They cannot:**
- Approve partnerships or pricing
- Make official statements on behalf of owners
- Handle serious bans or conflicts alone
- Promise anything to outside partners

**Escalation path:**
- Content questions → Media Lead
- Conflict or community issues → Positivity Lead
- Partnerships, money, official statements → Marcus or Milton

### 5. Tools and access (10 min)

- Instagram accounts and login process
- TikTok accounts and login process
- Discord server and moderation permissions
- Where to find media to post
- Group chat or communication channel with the team

### 6. First assignment (5 min)

Give them their first concrete task:

> **Audit current social presence.** List all RSS and RNG Instagram, TikTok, and Discord accounts. Note access status, last post date, follower count, and any immediate issues.

### 7. Questions and check-in schedule (5 min)

- Answer their questions.
- Schedule check-ins: Day 3, Day 7, Day 14, Day 30.
- End with encouragement.

## Reduced-scope option

If the person cannot commit 5–8 hours/week, reduce the scope to one of these:

- **RSS only** or **RNG only** (not both)
- **Instagram + Discord only** (skip TikTok)
- **Engagement only** (respond to comments/DMs) while someone else posts media

The role can grow over time as the person gets faster and more confident.
