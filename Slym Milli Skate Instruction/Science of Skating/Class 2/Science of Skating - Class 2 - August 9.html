🧦 Active Sock-Skating Warm-Up 2:30 – 2:35 (5 min)

🎯 Purpose: Warm up legs actively before static stretching.
  1. 🧦 Sock skate down and back — mindful skating walk, diagonal pushes, direction changes every 30–60 sec.
  2. ↔️ Lateral sliding down and back — open one leg by sliding it out to the side, then slide the other leg closed. Keep knees soft; focus on lateral hip mobility and controlled weight transfer.
  3. ⬅️ Move backward down and back — one foot at a time, deliberate weight shifts.
  4. 🔄 Sock-skate pivots — light toe-heel pivots to warm ankles, knees, hips.

📊 Position Levels Introduction 2:45 – 2:50 (5 min)

🎯 Purpose: Introduce the 6-position skating stance system so students understand how knee bend and center of gravity affect balance and control.

📏 What the 6 Levels Mean

  • Level 5: Standing straight up with legs locked. This is not a skating position; it is the top of the range.
  • Level 4 — Barstool position: A relaxed cruising stance. Used for rolling around and conserving energy.
  • Level 3 — Catcher stance: A moderate, athletic knee bend — the default stance for most rhythm skating.
  • Level 0: Sitting on the ground or on the back of the legs. This is not a skating position; it is the bottom of the range and a rest position.
  • Levels 2 and 1 exist between Level 3 and Level 0 for finer gradations.

🎯 What Students Will Practice

  • Find Level 5, then lower into Level 4 and Level 3.
  • Feel the difference between a locked, high stance (Level 5), a relaxed cruising stance (Level 4), and an athletic rhythm stance (Level 3).
  • Return briefly to Level 5 only to feel the contrast, then settle back into Level 3.
  • Note: This class only covers Levels 5 through 3. Levels below 3 will be introduced later.

🦶 Intro to Toe Manuals 2:50 – 2:55 (5 min)

🎯 Purpose: Introduce the toe manual as a balance on the back two wheels of one foot.

🧍 Setup

  • Done off skates (in socks) at a position between Level 3 and Level 4 (around 3.5).
  • Take a split stance: the toe of your back foot is just behind or in line with the heel of your front foot.
  • Evenly distribute your weight between both feet to start.

🎯 Drill Progression

  1. Front foot toe manual — raise the heel of the front foot so only the back two wheels touch, while the back foot stays flat. Lower and repeat.
  2. Back foot toe manual — raise the heel of the back foot while the front foot stays flat. Lower and repeat.
  3. Both heels — raise both heels at the same time so both feet are on their back two wheels, then lower them together.
  4. Switch foot positions — put the opposite foot in front and run through steps 1–3 again.

🎚️ Three Levels

  • Level 1 (Beginner): Perform the lifts slowly, using a wall or partner for balance if needed.
  • Level 2 (Intermediate): Perform the lifts without support, keeping eyes forward and core tight.
  • Level 3 (Advanced): Add a small knee bounce or shift weight forward and back while maintaining the split stance.

🔄 Class 1 Review 3:05 – 3:10 (5 min)

🎯 Purpose: Briefly revisit Class 1 fundamentals with a focus on weight transfer and stepovers before adding new material.
  1. 🦶 One-foot balance drill — shift hip over each leg, transferring weight 0–100%.
  2. ↗️ Add a front stepover — from the one-foot balance position, step the free foot in front of the supporting foot, transfer weight, and bring the original foot behind.
  3. ⬅️ Add a back stepover — from the one-foot balance position, step the free foot behind the supporting foot, transfer weight, and bring the original foot in front.
  4. 💡 Keep knees bent, core engaged, and weight fully committed to the supporting foot before each step.

🦶 Skated Toe Manuals 3:10 – 3:18 (8 min)

🎯 Purpose: Reintroduce the toe manual progression now on skates, transferring the off-skates balance work to rolling movement.

🧍 Setup

  • Done on skates at a position between Level 3 and Level 4 (around 3.5).
  • Take a split stance: the toe of your back skate is just behind or in line with the heel of your front skate.
  • Evenly distribute your weight between both skates to start.
  • Begin stationary or with very slow rolling movement.

🎯 Drill Progression

  1. Front skate toe manual — lift the heel of the front skate so only the back two wheels touch, while the back skate stays flat. Lower and repeat.
  2. Back skate toe manual — lift the heel of the back skate while the front skate stays flat. Lower and repeat.
  3. Both heels — lift both skate heels at the same time so both skates are on their back two wheels, then lower them together.
  4. Switch skate positions — put the opposite skate in front and run through steps 1–3 again.

🎚️ Three Levels

  • Level 1 (Beginner): Perform the lifts while stationary, holding a wall or partner if needed.
  • Level 2 (Intermediate): Perform the toe manuals while alternating which foot is in front.
  • Level 3 (Advanced): Add very slow rolling while maintaining the manual lifts.
