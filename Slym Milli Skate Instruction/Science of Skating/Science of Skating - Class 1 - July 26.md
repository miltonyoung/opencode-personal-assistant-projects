## Part 1 — Off-Skates: Concept of Skating Movement | 2:35 – 2:50 (15 min)

1. **Normal walk** down the floor.
2. **Mindful walk.** Notice how the foot hits the ground.
3. Ask: does the **toe or heel hit first?**
4. Ask: “If I asked you to walk faster, how would you do it?”
   - Expected answers:
     - Move feet faster.
     - Push the moving foot further each step.
5. **Explain the main difference.**
   - Walking pushes the foot straight forward.
   - Skating pushes the foot diagonally to the side.
   - Legs open, then close the gap.
   - Repeat to the other side.
6. **Practice the skating walk.**
   - Push diagonally out.
   - Close the gap.
   - Push to the other side.
7. **Add the toe pivot.**
   - Foot 1 pushes diagonally away from Foot 2.
   - Foot 2 pivots on its toe.
   - Heel swings inward.
   - Back wheels catch friction.
8. **Explain the weight shift.**
   - Start at 50/50.
   - Shift to 70/30 on the opening foot.
   - Close at 100/0 on the opening foot.

## Part 2 — Off-Skates: One Foot Balance Drill | 2:50 – 2:57 (7 min)

**Purpose:** Train the body to shift weight 0–100% on each foot.

#### Setup
- Feet shoulder-width apart.
- Tighten the core.
- Bend the knees.
- Lower the center of gravity.
- Do not over-hinge at the hip.
- Keep the back aligned and mostly straight.

#### Movement
- Shift weight from foot to foot.
- Push the hip over each leg.
- Outer hip lines up with the outside of the skate.
- Some bodies need the hip slightly past the skate.
- Bigger bodies may need a larger hip shift than smaller ones.

## Part 4 — Skated: Level 0 Pivot Drill | 3:05 – 3:17 (12 min)

#### Starting Position
- Feet side by side.
- Closer than shoulder-width.
- Knees bent.
- Weight balanced.

#### Movement
- Right foot pivots on the toe.
- Heel pulls inward.
- Aim for roughly 90 degrees.
- Left foot shifts forward in a straight line.
- Finish with right and left legs at 90 degrees.
- Skates should be very close, possibly touching.
- Pivot right foot again.
- Heel swings outward.
- Return to normal position.
- Pull left leg back in line.
- Left leg ends parallel with right.
- Repeat on the other side.

## Part 5 — Skated: Revisit Skate Fundamental Movement | 3:17 – 3:25 (8 min)

- Diagonal push to the side.
- Toe pivot on the supporting foot.
- Weight shift: 50/50 → 70/30 → 100/0.
- Close the gap.
- Repeat on the other side.

## Part 6 — Class Split: Explain + Optional Practice/Homework | 3:25 – 3:32 (7 min)

#### Path A — Beginners needing more time
- Keep practicing main skate fundamental drills.
- Focus on smooth stride.
- Focus on toe pivot.
- Focus on weight shift.
- Focus on clean closing.

#### Path B — Beginners ready to advance + higher levels
- Return to Skated One Foot Balance Drill.
- Lift the opposite foot off the ground.
- Raise the knee to 90 degrees.
- Hold, then switch sides.

#### Homework
- If time runs short, explain both paths.
- Assign the advanced one-foot balance variation for home practice.
