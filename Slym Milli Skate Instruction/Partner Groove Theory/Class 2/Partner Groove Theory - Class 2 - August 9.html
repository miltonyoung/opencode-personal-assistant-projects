🧦 Active Sock-Skating Warm-Up 4:00 – 4:05 (5 min)

🎯 Purpose: Warm up legs actively before static stretching. Reinforce skating fundamentals, balance transitions, and pushing through the feet.
  1. Sock skate down and back — continuous mindful skating walk.
    Push diagonally to the side. · Close the gap. · Transfer weight fully onto the opening foot. · Call direction changes every 30–60 seconds.
  1. Lateral sliding down and back — open one leg by sliding it out to the side, then slide the other leg closed.
    Keep knees soft and core engaged. · Focus on lateral hip mobility and controlled weight transfer.
  1. Move backward down and back — one foot at a time.
    Shift weight deliberately. · Keep knees soft and core engaged.
  1. Sock-skate pivots — light toe-heel pivots on the floor.
    Small range of motion. · Focus on warming ankles, knees, and hips.

👋 Introductions 4:05 – 4:08 (3 min)

🎯 Purpose: Welcome everyone, set the tone, and briefly preview the class focus.
  1. Welcome students by name if possible.
  2. Quick round-robin: name, favorite part of Class 1, one goal for today.
  3. Preview: today we move. Less talking, more skating.
  4. Remind students to switch partners whenever instructor calls it.

🧘 Static Stretching 4:08 – 4:18 (10 min)

🎯 Purpose: Stretch warm muscles safely before putting on skates. Keep it focused and moving.

🛼 Skate Up + Roll Around the Floor 4:18 – 4:23 (5 min)

🎯 Purpose: Give students time to put on skates and transition into movement immediately.
  • Students put on skates at their own pace.
  • As soon as skated, begin rolling around the floor.
  • Instructor circulates and gives light reminders about stride fundamentals.
  • No formal instruction yet — just movement and reset into the skating body.

🔄 Class 1 Review: Push-Pull + Stationary Turns + STL Ballroom 4:23 – 4:33 (10 min)

🎯 Purpose: Revisit Class 1 fundamentals quickly, keep students switching partners, and end with a full STL ballroom demo/attempt.
  1. Push-Pull demo — 30 sec
    Lead offers gentle pressure forward; follow matches it. · Reverse: lead pulls back; follow yields without collapsing frame.
  1. Push-Pull practice — 1.5 min
  1. Partner switch + Push-Pull practice — 1.5 min
  1. Turn 1: Pull-Through demo — 30 sec
    Lead pulls follow through a simple stationary turn. · Maintain connection through the hand/arm frame.
  1. Turn 1 practice — 1.5 min
  1. Partner switch + Turn 1 practice — 1.5 min
  1. Turn 2: Pull-Through + Reverse Catch demo — 30 sec
    Lead pulls follow through; follow turns back the other way and lead catches.
  1. Turn 2 practice (30 sec per partner) — 1 min
  1. Turn 3: Pull-Through Behind the Back demo — 30 sec
    Lead passes the follow’s hand behind their own back; follow turns underneath.
  1. Turn 3 practice (30 sec per partner) — 1 min
    Beginners may spend extra time here on simplified versions.
  1. STL Ballroom full demo + attempt + Q&A — 1.5 min
    Demo the full STL ballroom look/feel at real speed. · Students attempt with current partner. · One key takeaway, then transition.

➡️ Intro to Forward Gliding 4:33 – 4:43 (10 min)

🎯 Purpose: Build the foundation for non-stationary partner movement by teaching smooth, controlled solo forward gliding.

Setup

  • All work is solo today. Partner connection will come in the next section.
  • Find Level 3–4 stance (athletic but relaxed).
  • Weight shifts 0–100% from foot to foot.

Drill Progression

  1. Solo glide down and back — push, hold the glide, close.
    2.5 min
  1. Add a slight sway/rock — let the upper body float over the legs.
    2.5 min
  1. Longer solo glide with tempo changes — speed up and slow down while staying balanced.
    2.5 min
  1. Solo glide with direction changes — gentle turns and curves while gliding.
    2.5 min

Three Levels

  • Level 1 (Beginner): Small pushes, frequent ground contact, use a wall lightly for stability.
  • Level 2 (Intermediate): Longer glides, relaxed upper body, consistent tempo.
  • Level 3 (Advanced): Add a small manual, edge roll, or direction change while gliding.

🌀 Skating Forward Together in Partner Position Around a Large Circle 4:43 – 5:03 (20 min)

🎯 Purpose: Develop partner awareness, pacing, and body control while moving together around a large circle.

Setup

  • Form pairs on the inside edge of a large circle.
  • Partners face the same direction, side by side.
  • Start with no contact and build connection gradually.

Drill Progression

  1. Walk it first — slow, deliberate steps around the circle.
    1 min
  1. Step 1: Side by side, no touching
    Match each other’s pace. · Try to hit the ground at the same time with each foot. · 3 min
  1. Partner switch
    1 min
  1. Step 2: Hold hands
    Same pacing and foot-timing goal, now with a light hand connection. · 3 min
  1. Partner switch
    1 min
  1. Step 3: Glide together
    Hold the glide side by side; match tempo and spacing. · 3 min
  1. Partner switch
    1 min
  1. Step 4: Hold each other’s waist
    Maintain close but relaxed frame; keep feet and glide synchronized. · 3 min
  1. Partner switch
    1 min
  1. Step 5: Put a leg in front of partner and sway back and forth
    One partner places a leg in front of the other; both sway together in rhythm. · Switch roles halfway through if comfortable. · 3 min
  1. Final partner switch + common correction
    1 min

🔄 Traveling Turns / Moving Around the Circle 5:03 – 5:13 (10 min)

🎯 Purpose: Transfer stationary turn mechanics into moving, non-stationary partner turns around the floor.

Setup

  • Same large circle as previous section.
  • Partners move forward together around the circle.

Drill Progression

  1. Traveling stepover turn — step the outside foot over the inside foot while rolling; switch weight cleanly.
    1.5 min
  1. Traveling pull-through turn — lead initiates a gentle pull; follow turns under the arm while both keep rolling.
    1.5 min
  1. Partner switch + repeat — 1.5 min
  1. Traveling behind-the-back pull-through — slower tempo, smaller circle.
    1.5 min
  1. Partner switch + repeat — 1.5 min
  1. Open travel practice with instructor float — students mix turns freely while instructor circulates.
    1.5 min
  1. Common correction + whole-class reset — 1 min

Three Levels

  • Level 1 (Beginner): Stick to simple stepover turns; keep both hands available for balance.
  • Level 2 (Intermediate): Add the pull-through with one-hand connection.
  • Level 3 (Advanced): Add the behind-the-back variation and speed changes.

⬅️ STL Style Backward Skating (Optional) 5:13 – 5:23 (10 min)

🎯 Purpose: Introduce the backward skating element of the STL ballroom style in a controlled, progressive way.
📱 Note: Note: This section is optional. Use only if the partner forward work and traveling turns finish on time and the class is ready for more.

Setup

  • Use a wall or partner for stability at first.
  • 🦵 Knees bent, weight over the balls of the feet.
  • Small, deliberate backward pushes.

Drill Progression

  1. Solo backward walk along the wall — short steps, feel edges.
    1.5 min
  1. Solo backward glide off the wall — push, glide, close.
    1.5 min
  1. Partner backward-to-backward facing drill — partners face each other, hold hands, one skates backward while the other skates forward; switch roles.
    2 min
  1. Partner switch + repeat — 2 min
  1. Add a backward pivot / half-turn — roll backward, pivot 180° to forward, roll forward; repeat.
    1.5 min
  1. Try a short STL sequence: forward travel → backward travel → turn — low pressure, instructor demos first.
    1.5 min

Three Levels

  • Level 1 (Beginner): Stay at the wall or hold both partner hands; prioritize balance and control.
  • Level 2 (Intermediate): Skate backward with one-hand or no-hand connection.
  • Level 3 (Advanced): Add backward glides with transitions and connection changes.

🌙 Static Cool-Down Stretch 5:13 – 5:18 (5 min)

🎯 Purpose: Structured recovery and muscle safety at the end of class.
  1. Calf stretches.
  2. Hip flexor stretches.
  3. Hamstring stretch.
  4. Quad stretch.
  5. Ankle circles.
  6. Deep breath and class close.

🎬 Open Practice + Short Rotating Feedback 5:18 – 5:25 (7 min)

🎯 Purpose: Keep students moving after cool-down and give everyone at least one quick coaching touch.
  1. Students free-skate, practicing forward glides, circle travel, turns, and backward work.
  2. Instructor pulls students/pairs aside one at a time for 30–45 seconds of targeted feedback.
  3. When a common correction appears, pause the whole group, demonstrate, and have everyone practice it together for 60 seconds.
  4. Song change signals the last 60 seconds of open practice.

🧹 Cleanup 5:25 – 5:30 (5 min)

🎯 Purpose: Transition out of class mode and leave the floor tidy.
  1. Ask students to start removing skates and gathering personal items.
  2. Quick floor check: cones, markers, water bottles, trash.
  3. Brief thank-you and reminder about upcoming classes or events.
  4. Instructor available for quick questions while packing up.
