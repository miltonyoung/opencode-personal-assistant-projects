## Pre-Class Notes
- Keep talking segments under 60 seconds; students should be moving while listening whenever possible.
- Use song changes as transition cues.
- Demonstrate 3 levels of every pattern and let students self-select.
- During advanced work, pull newest skaters aside for foundational/simplified variations.

- Partner switching is woven throughout — never leave students in one partnership too long.

## Active Sock-Skating Warm-Up | 4:00 – 4:05 (5 min)

**Purpose:** Warm up legs actively before static stretching. Reinforce skating fundamentals, balance transitions, and pushing through the feet.

1. **Sock skate down and back** — continuous mindful skating walk.
   - Push diagonally to the side.
   - Close the gap.
   - Transfer weight fully onto the opening foot.
   - Call direction changes every 30–60 seconds.

2. **Lateral sliding down and back** — open one leg by sliding it out to the side, then slide the other leg closed.
   - Keep knees soft and core engaged.
   - Focus on lateral hip mobility and controlled weight transfer.

3. **Move backward down and back** — one foot at a time.
   - Shift weight deliberately.
   - Keep knees soft and core engaged.

4. **Sock-skate pivots** — light toe-heel pivots on the floor.
   - Small range of motion.
   - Focus on warming ankles, knees, and hips.

## Class 1 Review: Push-Pull + Stationary Turns + STL Ballroom | 4:23 – 4:33 (10 min)

**Purpose:** Revisit Class 1 fundamentals quickly, keep students switching partners, and end with a full STL ballroom demo/attempt.

1. **Push-Pull demo** — 30 sec
   - Lead offers gentle pressure forward; follow matches it.
   - Reverse: lead pulls back; follow yields without collapsing frame.

2. **Push-Pull practice** — 1.5 min

3. **Partner switch + Push-Pull practice** — 1.5 min

4. **Turn 1: Pull-Through demo** — 30 sec
   - Lead pulls follow through a simple stationary turn.
   - Maintain connection through the hand/arm frame.

5. **Turn 1 practice** — 1.5 min

6. **Partner switch + Turn 1 practice** — 1.5 min

7. **Turn 2: Pull-Through + Reverse Catch demo** — 30 sec
   - Lead pulls follow through; follow turns back the other way and lead catches.

8. **Turn 2 practice (30 sec per partner)** — 1 min

9. **Turn 3: Pull-Through Behind the Back demo** — 30 sec
   - Lead passes the follow’s hand behind their own back; follow turns underneath.

10. **Turn 3 practice (30 sec per partner)** — 1 min
    - Beginners may spend extra time here on simplified versions.

11. **STL Ballroom full demo + attempt + Q&A** — 1.5 min
    - Demo the full STL ballroom look/feel at real speed.
    - Students attempt with current partner.
    - One key takeaway, then transition.

## Intro to Forward Gliding | 4:33 – 4:43 (10 min)

**Purpose:** Build the foundation for non-stationary partner movement by teaching smooth, controlled solo forward gliding.

### Setup
- All work is solo today. Partner connection will come in the next section.
- Find Level 3–4 stance (athletic but relaxed).
- Weight shifts 0–100% from foot to foot.

### Drill Progression
1. **Solo glide down and back** — push, hold the glide, close.
   - 2.5 min

2. **Add a slight sway/rock** — let the upper body float over the legs.
   - 2.5 min

3. **Longer solo glide with tempo changes** — speed up and slow down while staying balanced.
   - 2.5 min

4. **Solo glide with direction changes** — gentle turns and curves while gliding.
   - 2.5 min

### Three Levels
- **Level 1 (Beginner):** Small pushes, frequent ground contact, use a wall lightly for stability.
- **Level 2 (Intermediate):** Longer glides, relaxed upper body, consistent tempo.
- **Level 3 (Advanced):** Add a small manual, edge roll, or direction change while gliding.

## Skating Forward Together in Partner Position Around a Large Circle | 4:43 – 5:03 (20 min)

**Purpose:** Develop partner awareness, pacing, and body control while moving together around a large circle.

### Setup
- Form pairs on the inside edge of a large circle.
- Partners face the same direction, side by side.
- Start with no contact and build connection gradually.

### Drill Progression
1. **Walk it first** — slow, deliberate steps around the circle.
   - 1 min

2. **Step 1: Side by side, no touching**
   - Match each other’s pace.
   - Try to hit the ground at the same time with each foot.
   - 3 min

3. **Partner switch**
   - 1 min

4. **Step 2: Hold hands**
   - Same pacing and foot-timing goal, now with a light hand connection.
   - 3 min

5. **Partner switch**
   - 1 min

6. **Step 3: Glide together**
   - Hold the glide side by side; match tempo and spacing.
   - 3 min

7. **Partner switch**
   - 1 min

8. **Step 4: Hold each other’s waist**
   - Maintain close but relaxed frame; keep feet and glide synchronized.
   - 3 min

9. **Partner switch**
   - 1 min

10. **Step 5: Put a leg in front of partner and sway back and forth**
    - One partner places a leg in front of the other; both sway together in rhythm.
    - Switch roles halfway through if comfortable.
    - 3 min

11. **Final partner switch + common correction**
    - 1 min

---

## Traveling Turns / Moving Around the Circle | 5:03 – 5:13 (10 min)

**Purpose:** Transfer stationary turn mechanics into moving, non-stationary partner turns around the floor.

### Setup
- Same large circle as previous section.
- Partners move forward together around the circle.

### Drill Progression
1. **Traveling stepover turn** — step the outside foot over the inside foot while rolling; switch weight cleanly.
   - 1.5 min

2. **Traveling pull-through turn** — lead initiates a gentle pull; follow turns under the arm while both keep rolling.
   - 1.5 min

3. **Partner switch + repeat** — 1.5 min

4. **Traveling behind-the-back pull-through** — slower tempo, smaller circle.
   - 1.5 min

5. **Partner switch + repeat** — 1.5 min

6. **Open travel practice with instructor float** — students mix turns freely while instructor circulates.
   - 1.5 min

7. **Common correction + whole-class reset** — 1 min

### Three Levels
- **Level 1 (Beginner):** Stick to simple stepover turns; keep both hands available for balance.
- **Level 2 (Intermediate):** Add the pull-through with one-hand connection.
- **Level 3 (Advanced):** Add the behind-the-back variation and speed changes.

## STL Style Backward Skating (Optional) | 5:13 – 5:23 (10 min)

**Purpose:** Introduce the backward skating element of the STL ballroom style in a controlled, progressive way.

> **Note:** This section is optional. Use only if the partner forward work and traveling turns finish on time and the class is ready for more.

### Setup
- Use a wall or partner for stability at first.
- Knees bent, weight over the balls of the feet.
- Small, deliberate backward pushes.

### Drill Progression
1. **Solo backward walk along the wall** — short steps, feel edges.
   - 1.5 min

2. **Solo backward glide off the wall** — push, glide, close.
   - 1.5 min

3. **Partner backward-to-backward facing drill** — partners face each other, hold hands, one skates backward while the other skates forward; switch roles.
   - 2 min

4. **Partner switch + repeat** — 2 min

5. **Add a backward pivot / half-turn** — roll backward, pivot 180° to forward, roll forward; repeat.
   - 1.5 min

6. **Try a short STL sequence: forward travel → backward travel → turn** — low pressure, instructor demos first.
   - 1.5 min

### Three Levels
- **Level 1 (Beginner):** Stay at the wall or hold both partner hands; prioritize balance and control.
- **Level 2 (Intermediate):** Skate backward with one-hand or no-hand connection.
- **Level 3 (Advanced):** Add backward glides with transitions and connection changes.
