7-Day Intermittent Fasting & Fueling Blueprint

Maximum fat loss while fueling extreme athletic output and preserving muscle mass.

Section 1: The Core Weekdays (Monday – Friday)

Monday – Friday

4:00 PM Lift or HIIT Class Daily
Calories: 2,240 Protein: 232g Carbs: 128g Fats: 86g
12:00 PM — Fast Breaker
The Supercharged Parfait

1.5 cups Kroger Carbmaster yogurt + 2 scoops whey protein isolate + 1 cup frozen berries.

470 Calories | 83g Protein

Breaks your 16-hour fast with an enormous protein payload that is semi-liquid. It digests quickly, ensuring your stomach is completely empty and comfortable by your 4:00 PM workout.

3:00 PM — Pre-Workout
The Liquid Primer

1 scoop whey protein isolate mixed with pure water.

110 Calories | 25g Protein

Floods your bloodstream with fast-acting amino acids to protect your muscle tissue during heavy lifting and high-intensity intervals without adding physical volume to your stomach.

4:00 PM – 6:00 PM
Gym Session

Lift or HIIT class.

6:15 PM — Recovery Dinner
The Heavy Refuel

20 oz (raw weight) 85/15 ground beef + 16 oz baked sweet potato (~1.5 potatoes).

1,600 Calories | 113g Protein

Your body is primed to handle the heavier fats from the 85/15 beef for sustained recovery. The massive sweet potato aggressively restores the glycogen depleted during your workout.

7:45 PM — Window Close
The Top-Off

1 cup Kroger Carbmaster milk.

60 Calories | 11g Protein

A zero-prep, liquid top-off to confidently secure your 230g protein goal right before your fasting window begins at 8:00 PM.

Section 2: The Rest Day (Saturday)

Saturday

No Gym | No Skating

12:00 PM
Parfait

3:00 PM
Skip This Completely

No pre-workout primer today.

6:15 PM
Dinner

20 oz 85/15 ground beef + 2 cups broccoli.

7:45 PM
Window Close Top-Off

1 cup Carbmaster milk + 1 scoop whey protein isolate.

Why Saturday is different

Because your physical output is vastly lower today, all heavy carbohydrates (sweet potatoes) are removed to accelerate fat burning. Protein remains fully maximized to repair the muscle damage from Monday through Friday.

Section 3: The Double-Day (Sunday)

Sunday

12:00 PM
Parfait + Oats

1.5 cups yogurt + 2 scoops whey + 1 cup berries + 1/2 cup dry oats mixed in.

3:00 PM
Skip This Completely

No pre-workout primer needed during the day.

6:15 PM
Dinner

1 cup Carbmaster milk + 1 scoop whey protein isolate.

Why Sunday is different

The added oats at noon provide complex, slow-burning carbs that stay in your system. Shifting the liquid whey to 7:45 PM guarantees your muscles are flooded with amino acids right as you step onto the rink for three hours.

A Final Note on Energy Levels

You are averaging a daily burn rate of 3,500 to 4,000 calories and eating roughly 2,240 calories. This creates an enormous deficit, allowing for rapid weight loss. If you experience severe exhaustion or a sudden drop in lifting strength mid-week, you have the safety buffer to easily add 300–500 calories of pure carbohydrates (like extra oats or rice) without slowing down your progress.
