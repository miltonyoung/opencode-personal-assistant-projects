# Immediate Next Steps

This is the action plan for the next 30, 60, and 90 days. These are the highest-priority tasks needed to move GenIdem from planning to revenue.

---

## The Big Picture

The most critical path to launching GenIdem is:

1. **Lock in the partnership.** (Legal / financial foundation)
2. **Build the MVP and prove it works.** (Technical foundation)
3. **Create marketing assets that sell the difference.** (Marketing foundation)
4. **Start pitching and booking events.** (Revenue foundation)

Do not build the kiosk or buy new gear until Stage 1 is proven with paid events.
