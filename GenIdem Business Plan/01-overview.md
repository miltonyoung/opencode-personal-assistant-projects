## The Core Concept

GenIdem delivers a photo booth experience that is closer to a studio portrait setup than a novelty iPad kiosk. By combining tethered capture, professional lighting, and a refined delivery workflow, the service offers:

- **High-end image quality** for weddings, galas, brand activations, and corporate events.
- **Visual marketing value** for B2B clients, turning every guest photo into branded, shareable content.
- **Operational efficiency** through unattended or lightly supervised deployments.

## Why This Market

The event photo booth market in Phoenix is crowded with low-cost iPad/tablet solutions. GenIdem's opportunity is to target clients who:

- Want superior image quality for their brand or event.
- Need a visual marketing asset, not just entertainment.
- Are willing to pay a premium for reliable, professional execution.

---

## Key Objectives

1. Launch a lean MVP within the next 8–12 weeks.
2. Build a portfolio and media kit that clearly communicates the quality difference.
3. Secure 3–5 paid events in the first quarter of operation.
4. Establish B2B partnerships with venues and corporate clients in Chandler/Tempe/Phoenix.
5. Progress to a fully unattended kiosk model once demand and revenue justify the investment.
