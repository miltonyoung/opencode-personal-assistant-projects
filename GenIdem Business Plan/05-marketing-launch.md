# Marketing & Launch Strategy

This document outlines how GenIdem will attract its first clients, build a premium brand, and grow through B2B relationships.

---

## Brand Positioning

**Tagline Ideas:**
- "Studio Quality. Booth Convenience."
- "A Photo Booth Built for Brands."
- "The Professional Photo Booth for Professional Events."

**Key Messages:**
- Professional studio gear (Canon R6, Godox strobe) — not an iPad.
- Affordable, high-quality photo booth rentals.
- Clean, polished images guests are proud to share.
- Built for private events and corporate activations in Chandler, Tempe, and Phoenix.

---

- Weddings

- Anniversary parties
- Family reunions

- Large corporate events with branded overlays and usage rights

### Ideal First Clients
- Venues and event planners who can refer repeat business.
- Corporate clients who value image quality and brand control.

## Launch Plan

### Phase 1: Launch Content Event

GenIdem's first marketing push will be a small, real-world **launch content event** (for example, a private party or styled gathering with friends/colleagues) rather than a staged studio shoot. This gives the portfolio and launch video authentic energy and lets guests experience the booth naturally.

**Event Goals:**
1. Capture portfolio-quality stills of real guests using the portrait bar.
2. Capture video of people using the booth, including reactions and social moments.
3. Record first-person POV interviews with guests about the experience.
4. Use all captured content as B-roll for the founder launch video.

**Content to Capture at the Event:**
1. **Guest portraits and candid booth shots**
   - High-resolution stills from the Canon EOS R6 setup.
   - Variety of poses, expressions, and group sizes.

2. **First-person POV interviews**
   - Short testimonials: "What did you think of the portrait bar?"
   - Authentic reactions and soundbites for Reels/TikTok/website.

3. **B-roll of the guest experience**
   - People walking up, pressing the trigger, reacting to the photo.
   - Social atmosphere: groups laughing, looking at the monitor, sharing photos.

4. **Behind-the-scenes / setup shots**
   - Gear build, lighting placement, monitor stand assembly.
   - Milton and Idara interacting with guests or adjusting the setup.

5. **Still-life gear shots**
   - Clean product photos of the setup for the media kit.

6. **Side-by-side quality comparison (optional)**
   - If available, same scene shot on an iPad/tablet booth vs. GenIdem.

**Props / Guest Direction:**
- Backdrop options
- A mix of casual and dressy guests so the portfolio covers different event types
- Champagne flutes or event-themed props
- Snacks, music, and lighting to make it feel like a real party
- Interview prompts prepared in advance
- Designated filming areas so the booth video does not feel staged

### Phase 2: Founder Launch Video + Media Kit + Website

**Founder Launch Video**

A short launch video hosted/filmed by Milton and Idara, using launch-event B-roll throughout.

**Suggested Structure:**
1. Hook: "What if your event photos actually looked like you hired a portrait studio?"
2. Introduce the GenIdem portrait bar and show it in action.
3. Explain why it exists: most booths are iPads; GenIdem is professional lighting + camera + a better guest experience.
4. Show guest reactions and POV interview clips.
5. CTA: how to book for weddings, corporate events, and brand activations.

**One-Page Media Kit Should Include:**
- GenIdem overview and differentiators
- Sample images from the launch content event
- Pricing tiers (or "starting at" pricing)
- Service area (Chandler / Tempe / Phoenix)
- Contact info and booking CTA

**Website Essentials:**
- Home: hero image + differentiator + founder intro video
- Gallery: portfolio images
- Services / Pricing
- About / Partnership story
- Contact / Booking form
- Google Business Profile linked and optimized

## B2B Visual Marketing Pitch

**Core Pitch to Venues / Corporate Clients:**

> "Most photo booths are fun but forgettable. GenIdem is a visual marketing tool — every guest photo is a branded, high-quality asset that lives on social media long after the event ends."

**What to Offer Venues:**

**What to Offer Corporate Clients:**
- Custom branded overlays.
- Custom or branded backdrop options (sourced through GenIdem's backdrop vendor).

- Usage rights for their social channels.
- Real-time gallery for event social walls.

## Outreach Strategy: Coffee Shops, Venues, and Event Planners

GenIdem will build early awareness by building relationships with local businesses and event professionals. The product is designed to sell itself through the launch content; outreach focuses on **referral relationships and paid opportunities**, not free demos.

### Target Partner Types

1. **Local Coffee Shops**
   - Build awareness by leaving a small branded display or QR-linked portfolio card.
   - Ask to be added to their vendor/community board if available.
   - Cross-promote on social channels without requiring a free activation.

2. **Event Venues**
   - Introduce GenIdem as a preferred vendor for their clients.
   - Leave behind a media kit and referral agreement.
   - Position the portrait bar as an upsell their clients can book directly.

3. **Event Planners / Coordinators**
   - Provide a "planner referral fee" (e.g., 10–15% of booked event revenue).
   - Invite planners to the launch content event so they can experience the product socially.
   - Offer a paid mini-shoot if they want headshots or team photos for their own brand.

### Outreach Tactics

| Tactic | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| In-person drop-in with tablet portfolio | Build familiarity; request a decision-maker's contact info |
| Email pitch + PDF media kit | Follow-up and formalize the offer |
| Referral commission agreement | Incentivize ongoing leads |
| Co-branded social content | Both parties post; expand reach organically |
| Launch content event invitation | Let partners experience the booth as guests, not prospects |

### First Outreach List

- [ ] 5–10 local coffee shops in Chandler / Tempe / Phoenix
- [ ] 10–15 event venues (wedding venues, conference centers, hotels, restaurants with private rooms)
- [ ] 10–15 event planners or corporate marketing coordinators
- [ ] 2–3 chambers of commerce or business networking groups

### Offer Package for Partners

**Referral Partnership:**
- GenIdem is available for the partner's clients at standard rates.
- Partner receives a 10–15% referral fee for any booked event they refer.
- Paid within 14 days after the referred event is completed and paid in full.
- No free activations; any live experience will be at a paid, contracted, or clearly defined co-marketing event.

**Co-Marketing Content (Optional):**
- If both parties want to produce shared content, define deliverables and costs in writing beforehand.
- GenIdem does not provide free booth hours for exposure alone.
