### LLC Formation Roadmap

**Entity:** New Arizona LLC for GenIdem, LLC

**Members:** Milton Young and Idara Ekpoh (50/50 ownership)

**Steps:**

1. **Reserve / Choose the LLC Name**
   - Confirm "GenIdem, LLC" or similar is available in Arizona.
   - Check Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC) name availability.
   - Optional: secure matching domain and social handles before filing.

2. **Hire an Attorney or Use an Online Filing Service**
   - Attorney route: recommended to ensure the Operating Agreement is tailored for a 50/50 partnership.
   - Self-file route: file Articles of Organization directly with the Arizona Corporation Commission.

3. **File Articles of Organization**
   - Arizona Corporation Commission online filing.
   - Cost: approximately $50–$85 filing fee (verify current fee).
   - Include: LLC name, statutory agent, principal address, member/manager info.

4. **Draft an Operating Agreement**
   - Must cover 50/50 ownership, profit/loss split, capital contributions, roles, decision-making, dispute resolution, and exit/buyout.
   - This is the most important document for the partnership.

5. **Obtain an EIN from the IRS**
   - Apply online at irs.gov (free).
   - Needed for the business bank account and tax filings.

6. **Open a Business Bank Account**
   - Use the Articles of Organization, EIN confirmation, and Operating Agreement.
   - Both Milton and Idara should have access according to their agreed roles.
   - Consider a bank with low fees, online access, and debit/credit card options.

7. **Register for State/Local Licenses if Required**
   - **Arizona Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT):** Arizona's version of sales tax. It is a tax on the privilege of doing business in the state. If GenIdem's photo booth rental and digital delivery services are subject to TPT, the LLC must collect and remit the tax based on the city/county where each event occurs. Rates vary by location (Chandler, Tempe, Phoenix, etc.).
   - Apply for an Arizona TPT license through the Arizona Department of Revenue before accepting payments if required.
   - City of Chandler or other local business license if applicable.

8. **Set Up Accounting**
   - Connect the bank account to QuickBooks, Wave, FreshBooks, or a spreadsheet.
   - Begin tracking all GenIdem income and expenses separately from personal or other business accounts.

## Contract Templates

**Budget:** $1,500 – $3,000 flat rate for a lawyer to draft two master contract templates.

### Template 1: B2C Agreement
**Use for:** Private events, weddings, parties

**Key Clauses:**
- Service description and hours.
- Package pricing and payment schedule.
- Cancellation and rescheduling policy.
- Gear liability and damage responsibility.
- Delivery timeline for images.
- Personal use rights for the client.
- Limitation of liability.
- Force majeure.

### Template 2: B2B Agreement
**Use for:** Corporate activations, brand events, venue partnerships

**Key Clauses:**
- Commercial usage rights (client, GenIdem, or shared).
- Data privacy and guest opt-in for marketing.
- Branding and watermarking requirements.
- Lead-capture data ownership.
- Indemnification.
- Payment terms (NET 15 / NET 30).
- Cancellation and deposit policy.
- Non-solicitation if working at a venue.

## Pricing Strategy

### Stage 1 MVP Pricing (Draft)

**Positioning:** Affordable, high-quality photo booth rental for clients who want better output than an iPad booth without paying premium luxury rates.

| Line Item | Rate / Minimum |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Hourly Rate** | $150 per hour |
| **Minimum Booking** | 2 hours ($300 minimum) |
| **Setup Fee** | $100 per event (covers the setup/teardown person's time) |
| **Attendant Fee** | $50 per hour if an attendant is required during the event |

### Example Event Pricing

| Event Type | Hours | Booth Fee | Setup Fee | Attendant | Total (excl. add-ons) |
|------------|-------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------------------|
| Small private party | 2 | $300 | $100 | — | $400 |
| Wedding reception | 4 | $600 | $100 | — | $700 |
| Corporate activation | 4 | $600 | $100 | + $200 (4 hrs) | $900 |

### Add-Ons
- Extra hour: $150
- Custom branded overlay: $100–$300
- Prints: cost + markup
- Additional attendant hours: $50/hour

### Pricing Principles
- Price must reflect the professional quality difference vs. iPad booths.
- Target clients who want affordable, high-quality options — not luxury pricing.
- Require a 50% deposit to confirm booking.
- Final payment due before or on event day.
- Setup fee and attendant fee are separate line items so clients see exactly what they are paying for.
- Setup fee covers one person arriving approximately one hour early to set up and returning at the end to tear down.

### Margin Targets
- Aim for at least 60–70% gross margin on labor-light events.
- Track time, mileage, editing, and gear depreciation to calculate true net margin.

## Financial Tracking

### Open a GenIdem, LLC Business Bank Account

**Why:** Separates GenIdem revenue and expenses from personal and other business accounts. Required once the LLC and EIN are in place.

**Documents typically required:**
- Articles of Organization (filed with Arizona)
- EIN confirmation letter from the IRS
- Operating Agreement
- Driver's licenses for Milton Young and Idara Ekpoh
- Initial deposit

**Account features to consider:**
- No monthly maintenance fee or low minimum balance
- Online banking and mobile check deposit
- Multiple debit cards (one for each partner)
- Ability to connect to accounting software
- Option for a business credit card later

**Timeline:** Open within 1–2 weeks after receiving the EIN.

### Accounting Categories
- Income: event bookings, add-ons, deposits
- Expenses: gear, insurance, software, marketing, legal, mileage, subcontractor pay, partner distributions

### Tools
- QuickBooks, Wave, FreshBooks, or a simple spreadsheet to start.
- Track every event's revenue, hours, and direct costs.
