## The Stage 1 MVP Setup

1. **Monitor stand** — central hub.
2. **Guest-facing monitor** mounted on the stand.
3. **Laptop stand** with Mac running Capture One or similar.
4. **Canon EOS R6** mounted via magic arm.
5. **Handheld trigger** so guests take their own photos.
6. **Separate C-stand** with Godox AD400Pro and beauty dish.
7. Operator stays nearby but the booth runs itself during capture.

## Launch Content Strategy

Before paid client work, GenIdem will host a small **real-party-style launch content event** at Idara's studio. The event should feel like an actual celebration, not a staged shoot, so guests relax and behave naturally on camera.

**Goals of the launch event:**
- Capture portfolio-quality stills of real people using the portrait bar.
- Capture video of guests using the portrait bar in a natural, social setting.
- Record first-person POV interviews with guests about the experience.

**Post-event content production:**
- Milton and Idara film a **founder intro video** explaining what GenIdem is, why it exists, and who it is for.
- Guest photos and video clips from the launch event will be used as **B-roll** in the intro video and across social content.

**Outreach / Demo Policy:**
- After the launch content is captured, GenIdem will not offer free activations.
- Coffee shops, venues, and planners are relationship and referral channels only.
- Any future demos will be paid, contracted, or part of a clearly defined co-marketing exchange.
