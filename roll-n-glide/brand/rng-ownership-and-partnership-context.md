# Roll N Glide — Ownership & Partnership Context

**Captured:** 2026-11-06

## How Roll N Glide relates to Slym Milli and Roller Skate Stories

- **Roll N Glide (RnG)** is its own entity / brand.
- It was originally started under **Slym Milli Entertainments**.
- **Slym Milli Entertainment** has become a **co-owner of Roller Skate Stories (RSS)**.
- As a result, **Roller Skate Stories and Slym Milli Entertainment** will collaborate on producing Roll N Glide events and Roll N Glide-related activities.
- Roll N Glide itself remains distinct; RSS and Slym Milli are the operating / event-producing partners.

## Historical timeline

- **Started:** Fall 2025, November.
- **Season cycle:** November → November (annual).
- **First initial meetup (this year):** November 21st.
- **Year 1 (2025–2026):** Milton ran all of RNG himself.
- **Year 2 (2026–2027):** Shifting toward sourcing personnel and involving the skate community — delegating roles and getting the community involved.

## Implications for planning

- Event budgets, staffing, and branding should account for the dual-partner structure.
- Partnership agreements, roles, and decision rights between RSS and Slym Milli should be documented before the first event.
- Shared assets (media, contacts, venues) should live in a place both partners can access.
- The November 21st meetup is the first delegated/community-involved event; roles and run-of-show need to be defined before then.
