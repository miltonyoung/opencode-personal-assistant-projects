# Roll N Glide — Event Operations Manual

**Season:** 2026–2027  
**First event:** November 21, 2026  
**Cadence:** Bi-weekly, Saturdays preferred (3:00–6:00 PM)  
**Location:** South Mountain Park area — details in first-timer navigation

---

## 1. Core Team Roles

Three paid/volunteer core roles are required for every event. Additional roles (Media, DJ, Vendor) are added as the event grows or as budget allows.

### Main Coordinator — Session Owner & Decision Maker

**Purpose:** Own the overall success, energy, and safety of the session.

**Key responsibilities**
- Confirm weather and post session status 24–48 hours prior.
- Arrive by 2:30 PM to scout the spot and set up the vibe.
- Welcome the group at 3:00 PM and set the inclusive tone.
- Keep the session flowing and address any issues that arise.
- Close the session at 5:45–6:00 PM with thanks, next-date preview, and group photo.
- Post a short recap or thank-you on Discord/IG after the event.
- Make final decisions on weather, timing, safety, and on-site issues.

**Timeline checklist**
- Day before: confirm weather, share coordinates, post reminder.
- 2:30 PM: arrive, confirm spot is clear, greet early arrivals.
- 3:00 PM: official welcome + introduce supports and any special activities.
- 3:20–5:30 PM: circulate, support energy, handle issues.
- 5:45 PM: group thank-you, next date, photo opportunity.
- After event: update planner and post recap.

---

### Support 1 — Welcome, Flow & Beginner Support

**Purpose:** Keep the session welcoming, organized, and beginner-friendly.

**Key responsibilities**
- Arrive by 2:45 PM and help with any light setup.
- Greet people as they arrive and point first-timers to the Main or coordinates.
- Support beginners and anyone who looks unsure.
- Help with partner skating intros or simple skill shares if planned.
- Watch group safety and hydration (especially in sun).
- Assist with cleanup and making sure no one is left behind at the end.

**Timeline checklist**
- 2:45 PM: arrive and check in with Main Coordinator.
- 3:00–3:20 PM: help welcome and pair people if needed.
- During session: float and support new or struggling skaters.
- 5:30–6:00 PM: help wrap up and gather the group.
- After event: share any notes or issues with the Main.

---

### Support 2 — Navigation, Ideas & Post-Event

**Purpose:** Handle first-timer navigation support and community feedback.

**Key responsibilities**
- Help share accurate coordinates and map pin the day before.
- Be available on Discord/IG for people who can’t find the spot.
- Collect community ideas during the session.
- Take a few photos or notes of highlights (with permission).
- Help with post-event recap notes and idea logging.
- Support cleanup and thank-yous.

**Timeline checklist**
- Day before: confirm coordinates are posted and accurate.
- During session: collect ideas and note any standout moments.
- 5:30–6:00 PM: help wrap up and gather feedback.
- After event: log ideas in the tracker and share notes with Main.

## 2. Optional / Growth Roles

These are not required for the first event, but should be added as soon as there is budget, equipment, or volunteer capacity.

### Media Team — Photos, Video & Community Storytelling

**Purpose:** Capture the vibe and moments of the session for the community.

**Key responsibilities**
- Arrive by 2:50 PM and check in with the Main Coordinator.
- Take photos and short video clips throughout the session.
- Focus on energy, connections, skill moments, and the mountain views.
- Always ask permission before close-up shots of individuals.
- Share a small selection of highlights with the Main after the session.
- Respect anyone who does not want to be photographed.

**Important notes**
- Quality over quantity — a few great moments beat dozens of random shots.
- Keep volume and movement respectful of the group and park.
- Content is for community building, not commercial use without approval.

---

### DJ Setup — Music & Atmosphere

**Purpose:** Provide the soundtrack that sets the Roll N Glide vibe (R&B, feel-good, skate-friendly).

**Key responsibilities**
- Arrive by 2:40 PM to set up and sound-check.
- Keep volume respectful of other park users and the group.
- Have a playlist ready that fits the community vibe.
- Be ready to lower volume or pause for announcements from the Main.
- Pack up cleanly by 6:00 PM so the group can exit on time.
- Coordinate with Main on any special music requests or moments.

**Important notes**
- Park rules and neighboring visitors matter — keep volume moderate.
- Have a backup playlist in case of technical issues.
- Confirm power source or battery needs with the Main ahead of time.

---

### Vendor — Food Truck or Merchandise

**Purpose:** Add value to the session (food, drinks, or community merch) without disrupting the skate flow.

**Key responsibilities**
- Confirm attendance and exact setup needs with the Main Coordinator in advance.
- Arrive early enough to be ready by 3:00 PM (or agreed time).
- Set up in a designated area that does not block skating space or pathways.
- Keep the area clean and respectful of the park.
- Be ready to pack up by 5:45–6:00 PM so the group can exit before gates close.
- Coordinate any special offerings or pricing with the Main.

**Important notes**
- Park rules and any required permits are the vendor’s responsibility.
- Setup location must be approved by the Main Coordinator.
- Leave the area cleaner than you found it.

## 4. First-Timer Navigation

**Park entrance:** 10409 S Central Ave  
**Spot notes:** To be confirmed and shared before each event.  
**Gate hours:** Plan to leave before 7:00 PM.  
**Silent Sunday reminder:** If skating on a Sunday, be aware the park runs Silent Sunday restrictions on some roads/trails — plan parking and route accordingly.  
**Safety tips for newcomers**
- Bring water, sunscreen, and a charged phone.
- Wear protective gear if still learning.
- Stay visible to the group; don’t wander off alone.
- Ask anyone for help — the team is here for that.

## 6. Weather & Cancellation Policy

- Confirm weather 24–48 hours before the event.
- If weather is unsafe (heavy rain, lightning, extreme heat, high winds), cancel or reschedule as early as possible.
- The Main Coordinator makes the final call.
- Communicate the decision on IG/Discord and update the season schedule immediately.
